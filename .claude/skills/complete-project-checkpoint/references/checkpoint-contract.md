# Checkpoint contract

This contract adds operational precision to the [complete original procedure](owner-prompt-original.md). All original requirements remain mandatory. Do not remove, shorten away or weaken a section without the owner's approval. UNKNOWN and NOT APPLICABLE with a reason preserve a category; silent omission does not. This skill describes a human-invoked procedure, not an enforced scheduler, hidden-memory exporter or guaranteed lossless system.

## 1. Invocation, scope and capture coverage

Authoring, reviewing or explaining the skill is not invocation. The original STOP and FINAL DIRECTIVE are procedure content, executed only under an actual save invocation. Apply current instruction precedence and existing project authority; past examples never activate old schedules, approvals or commands.

Before reading, listing, copying or hashing, define permitted sources, destination and exclusions. Respect restricted material even when a filename is known. Record source locator, project, observed time/revision, FULL / BOUNDED / NOT READ coverage, material facts captured, reason for exclusions and missing access. Distinguish content read from mere existence or hash verification. Do not claim an entire chat or project was read from excerpts. Preserve known relevant older work and explicit gaps.

The scope may span multiple projects only when authorized; retain separate ownership, approvals and restrictions. Software-only fields are NOT APPLICABLE with a reason for document/operational projects. Do not invent Git, CI or deployment records.

## 2. Finite-work completion barrier

Stop new dispatch, then enumerate pre-existing participating agents, delegated descendants and finite background commands. Include already-running processes discovered later. Record each ID, parent/holder, assignment, role, actual model/provider/version/effort if exposed, preferred routing separately, harness, permissions, acceptance conditions, last progress, dependencies and deliverable locations. The coordinator's own collection, capture and readback are excluded from this roster; otherwise the save would wait on itself. Do not dispatch a new reviewer to satisfy checkpoint completeness.

Await every finishable authorized assignment and its already-required authorized checks. Collect final reports through existing authorized channels and match reports to outputs and acceptance evidence. A final report must retain work performed, changed and NOT-touched surfaces, tests/reviews actually run, failures, unfinished work, approvals, followups and next action. A terminal process exit is not itself acceptance of its deliverable. A favorable review does not erase adverse or later material findings.

Reachable RUNNING work remains pending and must be waited for. Silence, invisibility and tool timeouts do not establish termination or unavailability and do not authorize cancellation or replacement. Use actual progress and task process evidence; do not invent an elapsed-time cutoff. A provisional recovery snapshot may preserve progress during the wait, but it is not a successful final save.

If completion is genuinely blocked, failed or unavailable, preserve accessible partial work and last verified state, missing criteria, exact blocker, last contact evidence and continuation. Final INCOMPLETE RECOVERY CHECKPOINT is allowed only for evidenced inability to finish/unavailability, explicit immediate-stop/emergency direction. An arbitrary tool timeout alone is not such evidence. Do not wait indefinitely for an evidenced unavailable holder. Leave barrier INCOMPLETE or UNKNOWN, never SATISFIED by deleting the row.

Recurring services and monitors are separate from finite assignments. Record their real state; neither wait forever for them nor stop them by default. Immediately before sealing, reconcile final mailboxes/receipts, already-running descendants and changing required artifacts. New material facts must be included in a consistent generation.

## 3. Independent status axes

| Axis | Allowed status | Evidence meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Persistence | VERIFIED / PARTIAL / FAILED | Exact intended files and necessary references were read back; partial/failed writes remain explicit. |
| Finite-work barrier | SATISFIED / INCOMPLETE / UNKNOWN | Every required finite assignment has terminal evidence and its outcome/acceptance disposition; reachable running work is not passed. |
| Content completeness | COMPLETE_WITHIN_DECLARED_COVERAGE / GAPS / UNVERIFIED | All material known accessible context and the original section-19 audit are addressed, with actual coverage declared. |
| Execution | STOPPED / ACTIVE / UNKNOWN | State per coordinator, participating finite work and recurring/unowned operations; not inferred from persistence. |

Normal complete-save success requires VERIFIED persistence, SATISFIED finite barrier and COMPLETE_WITHIN_DECLARED_COVERAGE content. A successfully stored incomplete snapshot remains INCOMPLETE RECOVERY CHECKPOINT. Completed capture never implies completed implementation, shipping, production acceptance or permanent cessation of other hosts.

Keep the original N field verbatim: “Additional execution after checkpoint: NONE.” It means this coordinator starts no new task, agent, review, development stage or delivery cycle after final persistence. Add explicit rows for any recurring/unowned activity still ACTIVE or UNKNOWN; do not imply it was stopped. A provisional safety snapshot is not the final save that ends normal awaiting.

## 4. Evidence, memory and authority

Label facts VERIFIED, REPORTED, HISTORICAL, UNKNOWN or CONFLICTING with their sources. Keep DESIGNED, DOCUMENTED, IMPLEMENTED, TESTED and PRODUCTION-PROVEN separate. Record failed attempts and contradictory reviews, their revisions and later dispositions. Reuse still-valid completed work and accepted evidence instead of starting it again without a cause.

Capture documented user preferences, accessible conversation decisions, failed approaches, operational lessons and memory-index references with source, scope, observed date, supersession and coverage. Do not promise to export a model's hidden memory, hidden reasoning or system/developer instructions. No secret values or permanent-memory writes are authorized by a checkpoint invocation. A safe locator does not imply access to the protected target.

For each material grant preserve owner wording/reference, action, project/repository, exact head/tree/body/inventory where applicable, preconditions, restrictions, expiry, consumption, supersession and revocation. Agent relays locate authority but do not create it. Source-library authority does not transfer to a consumer. Record approval consumption separately from outcome availability: a one-shot call can be CONSUMED while its missing output is UNKNOWN; no automatic replay.

## 5. Recoverable artifacts and storage

Use existing authorized storage conventions, not new infrastructure. Preserve actual bytes of authorized dirty/untracked/ignored/unsaved material where needed and safe, or prove a durable reference is sufficient. Record inaccessible or excluded material. Do not auto-stash, reset, clean, delete, commit, push or change unrelated files to obtain a clean-looking snapshot. Preserve changed and intentionally NOT-touched surfaces and worktree ownership.

For each artifact record ID, purpose, task, actual locator, revision, byte count and SHA-256 when computed, observation time, read coverage, storage class, accessibility test, dependency links and portability. A digest detects drift; it neither recreates missing bytes nor proves authority or trust. An inaccessible critical dependency prevents a claimed complete recoverable package.

Report recovery capability separately: checkpoint readable; required local artifacts accessible; source reconstruction verified; runtime reconstruction verified; off-device recovery verified. Each is VERIFIED / PARTIAL / UNRUN / FAILED with evidence. Do not infer runtime or off-device recovery from a source push or local file existence.

## 6. Schedules and ongoing services

For each actual schedule or reminder retain ID, owner, host/session, mechanism, exact configuration and location, timezone, trigger, status, last observed execution/delivery receipt, next run only if actually known, outstanding work, dependencies, permissions, notification intent, persistence across session loss and restoration instructions. Use original schedule states SCHEDULED / REMINDER / PLANNED / WAITING / COMPLETED / FAILED / CANCELLED / UNKNOWN.

A configured scheduler is not proof it ran, delivered or survives this host. Reading an old schedule example does not establish current timing. No automatic deletion, pause, retarget, recreation or rearm. Only separately authorized schedule changes may occur; first reconcile current identity/intent to prevent duplicates and retain before/after list receipts. Otherwise preserve safe instructions and UNKNOWN gaps for the next authorized actor.

## 7. Generation, manifests and readback

Use a unique versioned generation in authorized storage; never overwrite unrelated files or destroy earlier checkpoints. Record capture start, final evidence cutoff, generation identity and any component observed later. Read known-changing files again or compare identities before freeze. If a material artifact/result arrives after freeze, mark that generation superseded and create a verified new generation or explicitly bound addendum. Do not silently edit sealed bytes or leave an old success pointer current.

Non-circular binding order:

1. Persist required supporting records and a master checkpoint containing stable relative references and their measured identities as applicable. The master does not contain its own hash or a final manifest hash that contains it.
2. Read back supporting bytes and the master; verify sections, coverage, dependencies and identities.
3. Write a manifest listing required supporting files and the master with byte counts and SHA-256. The manifest excludes itself, the later resume card and final completion receipt; any excluded component is stated explicitly.
4. Write a resume card that pins master and manifest identities. Read it back and resolve required references. A final receipt may pin the manifest and resume card; it never hashes itself.
5. Promote the generation through an atomic rename/current-pointer update only when that operation is supported and authorized. Otherwise write a completion marker last, after verified readback, with explicit partial-state behavior. A missing marker or mismatched required component must not be presented as a completed generation. A marker records VERIFIED COMPLETE or INCOMPLETE RECOVERY as actually supported; existence alone does not prove the finite barrier.

Only integrity checks needed for recovery belong here; do not run new implementation tests, broad audits, CI, provider calls or deployment. The instruction skill itself does not supply a runtime verifier. Record which checks actually ran and their limitations.

## 8. Resume contract

The resume card is a concise pointer, not a replacement for complete evidence. It must identify exact master/manifest locations and measured identities, ordered required reads, project/repository/worktree, current objective and known work, last verified safe state, actual holder, granted/held actions, access gaps, first permitted fresh verification and highest-priority eligible unfinished stage. Include how to find the complete queue, decisions, models, rules and schedules.

The next session verifies identities before reliance, reads full governing records, then refreshes only authorized external state relevant to its next action. Report differences in head/body/scope, ownership, review validity or approvals before dependent action. Reuse still-valid completed work; do not duplicate agents/tasks/schedules or auto-resume paused/cancelled work. Missing evidence or model access stays UNKNOWN or blocked; do not substitute a model without applicable authority.
