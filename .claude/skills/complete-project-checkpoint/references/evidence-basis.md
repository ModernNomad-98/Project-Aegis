## Why the additions exist

These additions operationalize original obligations. Historical samples informed the design; their full local investigation and private project records remain outside this portable skill. They are not required runtime dependencies and do not supply present-day authority, timings or project status.

| Addition | Original requirement | Generalized historical observation | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Finite agent AND process barrier | Sections 2–5, 19 | A checkpoint recorded an unfinished finite background command while also claiming agents were finished. | Agent visibility alone cannot prove all finite work finished. |
| Recoverable dirty/untracked bytes | Sections 4, 13, 16, 20–21 | Historical recovery records preserved untracked work and change copies separately. | A path or source revision alone may not reconstruct local work; no destructive cleanup implied. |
| Memory provenance and coverage | Sections 16–18 | Handoffs referenced documented memory/context sources and unavailable conversations. | Preserve accessible documented material and gaps; do not invent hidden memory export. |

| Short pinned resume card | Phase 7 | Historical prompts supplied checkpoint hashes, ordered reads and exact next actions. | The card points to full detail; it does not replace the checkpoint. |
| Approval lifecycle and consumed actions | Sections 11–12, 18 | A record distinguished a consumed action from its unavailable result; resumed records changed holders after fresh evidence. | Missing output does not reset a one-shot grant; do not duplicate work or transfer source grants. |

## Limits and evidence reuse

These observations justify explicit fields and checks, not a claim that this procedure has reduced measured time, tokens or cost. No universal losslessness, automatic host enforcement, production proof, persistent scheduling or off-device recovery is claimed. The skill's current cases are synthetic authored probes; their presence or JSON validity is not a passing behavioral result. Preserve actual run receipts separately.

Existing handoff-design, startup, memory-governance and one-task closeout skills retain their different roles. The earlier immediate-stop fast-save protocol and synthetic delivery-control helper are not replaced or modified by this skill. No section removal is authorized. All project-specific source grants and historical schedule examples remain historical until verified for an actual invocation.
