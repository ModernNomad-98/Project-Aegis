# Share your experience with this starter package

This package is meant to help you get from an idea to a delivered project. Your experience is how it improves. Please tell us what worked, what confused you, and where you got stuck — a few lines is enough.

## What to send

Use docs/consumer-feedback-template.md as a starting point (copy the questions into your message; no need to answer everything). Please include:

- what you were trying to do, and the stage where it happened (discover, plan, build, validate, deliver);
- what you expected, what happened instead, and what it cost you (time, an extra question, a redo);
- anything that worked noticeably well;
- your experience level, if you are comfortable sharing it (first project, some coding, experienced);
- the host and version you used, if you know it (do not include device names or personal paths).

Please do not send: real names, contact details of other people, account details, passwords, health information, photos or videos, or anything about a child.

## How to send it

- Send it through the repository's public issue tracker: https://github.com/ModernNomad-98/Project-Aegis/issues — open an issue and put your experience in it. This is the only feedback route (owner decision, 2026-11-15); no email address is published.)

## What happens to it

Your feedback is read and, where it is generally useful, recorded in the project's improvement log (docs/aegis-feedback-log.md) with identifying detail removed (docs/aegis-feedback-anonymized.md). It is used to improve the process and the package, never sold or shared beyond that. The anonymized improvement log is never sent anywhere; passing it on is the owner's decision.

## Privacy

Do not include personal data in feedback. If you include it by mistake, say so and it will be removed. Never include another person's details without their permission.
