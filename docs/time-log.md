# Owner time log

**Method.** Owner time is counted from the times of the owner's messages, in the owner's local time zone (America/Los_Angeles). A gap of more than 30 minutes between messages is a break and is not counted; shorter gaps are counted. If more than one chat works on the project, merge the message times into one timeline; never add the chats together.

**Record status for this project.** The exact times of the owner's messages are not retained in the visible session record; only sampled turn times are available. No measured total is claimed, and no figure is invented.
